Simple, transparent payments

Every order begins with a quotation outlining the estimated cost of items, our service fee and any applicable delivery charges. Once you approve the quotation and complete payment, we purchase the requested items or begin your errand. Receipts are provided where applicable, and any difference between estimated and actual costs is reconciled transparently. For recurring monthly orders, payment arrangements and delivery dates are agreed in advance.

A few helpful things

Questions people ask.

Still wondering about something? We’re just a WhatsApp message away.

Ask us directly ↗
Which countries can I order from?

Anywhere. You can pay online in your own currency, and we take care of the errand in Kenya.

Where do you deliver?

We’re starting in Nairobi and surrounding areas. Share the delivery area in your order and we’ll confirm if we can reach it.

Can you buy prescription medicine?

Yes, with a valid prescription. We buy from licensed pharmacies and never substitute a product without your approval.

What if the price changes or something is out of stock?

We’ll message you with the options first. Nothing is swapped or added without your say-so.

How do I pay?

Payment options include card, PayPal or bank transfer in USD, GBP, EUR, AED or KES. Payment details are shared with your quote.
